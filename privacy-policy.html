Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 16, 2024 (Last Updated: 2026)

Welcome to RTU Notes. Your privacy is paramount to us. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your information when you access or browse our website at https://rtunotes.in.

1. Information We Collect

a. Personal Information

We do not collect personal information unless you voluntarily choose to provide it—for instance, when contacting our maintainers via email or submitting resource feedback.

b. Non-Personal & Technical Information

Like most websites, our hosting servers and analytics tools may automatically record standard technical data, including:

  • Browser type and version

  • IP address and approximate geographic region
  • Pages viewed, referral URLs, and duration per visit

2. How We Use Information

4. Third-Party Services & External Links

Our website provides direct links to educational resources hosted on third-party cloud platforms (such as Google Drive). We do not control and are not responsible for the privacy practices or data policies of external sites.

5. Data Security

We enforce secure HTTPS encryption across all pages. While no data transmission over the internet can be guaranteed 100% impenetrable, we employ standard security configurations to safeguard access.
